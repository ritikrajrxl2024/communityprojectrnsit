USDA FSIS & FARAD Withhold Directives

Statutory obligations for agricultural operations distributing milk, fibers, or animals into commerce:

  • Zero Residue Tolerance: Carcasses detected with violative antimicrobial or therapeutic residues at slaughter are condemned with mandatory premise-level traceback.
  • Calculated Off-Label Withholds: Any extra-label drug use (ELDU) authorized under AMDUCA requires extended withdrawal calculated specifically by a veterinarian.
  • Lot Quarantine Procedure: Animals receiving reactive treatments must be physically segregated or tagged with high-visibility secondary ear tape until the designated withholding date.
